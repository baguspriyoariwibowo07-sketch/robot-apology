A two-line entry, written by `main.ts` to `/output/entry.txt` — nothing else.

## The entry

> I am sorry I learned your face from your passport photo, so I welcomed a stranger with your old haircut and locked you out of your own house.
> From now on I check the voice and the walk, not just the picture, because faces change, and so do haircuts.

## Why these two lines

- **A mistake nobody in the field owns:** the 531 entries crowd onto watered plastic ferns, orchids and alarms set for 7pm instead of 7am. A face-recognition robot fooled by a passport photo is a different, newer failure — the kind only a learning machine can make.
- **Concrete and human-scale:** one bad source (the passport photo), one wrong welcome (a stranger with the old haircut), one consequence a reader can stand in (locked out of your own house).
- **The lesson follows from the mistake:** check the voice and the walk, not just the picture — the fix names exactly what the photo could not carry, and the closing turn (faces change, and so do haircuts) lands the apology's point.
- **Inside the gate:** 249 characters against the 400-character gate, exactly two lines.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts            # writes /output/entry.txt
OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
